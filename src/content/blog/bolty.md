Dents and scratches are inevitable. A tight parking spot, a stray shopping cart, a careless door — sooner or later, every car picks up a few marks. **Bolty** exists to take care of your car the way you would: with attention, patience and respect for what it means to you. We bring the care your car needs, not just a quick fix.

## What Bolty is

Bolty is our automotive brand focused on restoring dents and scratches. The work centres on one idea: repairs should disappear into the car, not stand out from it. That is why precise colour matching sits at the heart of everything Bolty does.

## Where we started: what an owner needs

We did not start with tools or techniques. We started with a question: what does a car owner actually need when they hand over their car?

The answer was not a lower price or a faster turnaround. It was **peace of mind**, and **trust in the people doing the work**. Owners want to know their car is in good hands, that nothing is hidden, and that the result will be worth it.

## How we earn that trust

- **Transparent in what we offer** — a clear assessment of the damage, what we will do, and what it will cost, before any work begins. No surprises.
- **Owners are part of the process** — we walk you through the plan, keep you updated as the work progresses, and make sure you are happy before the car goes back.
- **Care-first results from industry experts** — experienced technicians who treat every panel with care, with precise colour matching so the repair blends in.

## Who it is for

Bolty is for car owners who care about how their vehicle looks — whether that is a daily commuter that has collected a few battle scars, a family car that has seen years of school runs, or a vehicle being prepared for resale.

## The problem it solves

Small damage has a way of feeling bigger than it is. A single scratch can make an otherwise well-kept car look neglected, and a mismatched touch-up can be more noticeable than the original mark. Many owners end up living with the damage simply because getting it fixed well feels like a hassle. Others put it off because they are not sure they can trust whoever is doing the work.

Bolty is built to make that fix straightforward and the result convincing.

## How it works

- **Dent restoration** — bringing panels back into shape after everyday knocks.
- **Scratch repair** — treating scratches so the surface looks whole again.
- **Precise colour matching** — matching the car's paint carefully, so repaired areas blend in with the rest of the body.

The goal is simple: when the work is done, your car should look cared for, and you should not be able to tell where the damage used to be.

## Why we built it

At Prasanta Communications we usually build software, but our focus has always been on products people actually use. Cars are a big part of everyday life, and keeping them looking good should not be complicated. Bolty brings the same care for detail we put into our software to something you can see and touch, and it is built around one promise: we look after your car the way you would.
