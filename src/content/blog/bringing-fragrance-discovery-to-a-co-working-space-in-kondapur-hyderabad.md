A workplace is not just about meetings, deadlines and getting work done. It is also about the people, experiences and small moments that make the day more enjoyable.

With this thought, ZONE OUT Parfum organised a fragrance discovery stall at a co-working space in Kondapur, Hyderabad.

![Coworking space](/images/posts/1.jpeg)

Our idea was simple: to bring our fragrance collection closer to people and give them an opportunity to explore and experience our perfumes in person.

## A Fragrance Experience at the Workplace

We set up a fragrance stall where workspace members could explore our perfume collection, discover different fragrances and try the products.

It was interesting to see people stop by, explore the collection and learn more about our fragrances. Some were curious to try something new, while others wanted to find a fragrance that suited their everyday lifestyle.

![Fragrance Experience](/images/posts/2.jpeg)

Our team interacted with visitors, introduced our products and helped them understand the different fragrances in our collection.


## A Good Response from the Members

The event received a good response from the people at the workspace. Visitors showed interest in our products, explored different fragrances and enjoyed the experience.

For us, it was a great opportunity to connect with people directly, understand their preferences and introduce ZONE OUT Parfum beyond the online shopping experience.

![Members interactions](/images/posts/3.jpeg)

These interactions helped us understand how people explore fragrances and what they look for when choosing one.


## A Special Co-branded Offering

Along with the fragrance stall, we also introduced a special co-branded offering exclusively for the co-working space members.

This made the experience more meaningful by bringing together the workspace community and ZONE OUT Parfum. It was a way to create something special for the members while giving them an opportunity to explore our fragrance collection.

![Co branding](/images/posts/4.jpeg)

We wanted the members to feel that this was more than just another product display. It was a special experience created for their workspace community.

## More Than Just a Fragrance Stall

This experience was not only about displaying and introducing perfumes. It was about creating a small moment of discovery during a regular workday.

We enjoyed meeting new people, listening to their fragrance preferences and seeing them explore our products.

At ZONE OUT Parfum, we believe fragrances are connected to personal moments, memories and everyday experiences. Events like these give us an opportunity to share that feeling with more people.

We look forward to creating more such experiences and connecting with different communities.
