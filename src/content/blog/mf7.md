Running a gym is two jobs at once. There is the work people see — coaching, motivating, and helping every member get a little stronger than they were last month. And there is the work they do not see: tracking who has paid, whose plan expires this week, who needs a nudge, and how the month is actually going. **MF7**, short for _Mind and Fitness 7_, is built to support both: it takes care of the admin, and it keeps the coaching relationship — and the progress clients care about — visible and on track.

## What MF7 is

MF7 is software-as-a-service built for gyms and fitness centres. It gives owners a single place to manage the business side of their gym and the journey of every client in it — from the day they sign up to the goals they are working towards. It comes with mobile apps for both owners and their clients, so the people who run the gym and the people who train there are working from the same source of truth, and owners can see at a glance how their members and their business are progressing.

## Who it is for

MF7 is designed for gym owners and fitness centre operators who have outgrown notebooks, scattered chats, and spreadsheets — but do not want heavyweight enterprise software either. If you know your members by name and want to keep it that way while the business grows, MF7 is built for you.

## The problems it solves

Most gyms run into the same handful of headaches:

- Member details living in too many places at once.
- Subscriptions quietly lapsing because nobody noticed the end date.
- Payments that are hard to reconcile at the end of the month.
- Sales and renewals that depend on someone remembering to follow up.
- Client progress that lives in a trainer's head or notebook — and walks out the door when the trainer changes.
- No clear picture of whether members are reaching their goals, or whether the gym itself is growing month over month.

MF7 brings these together so the day-to-day admin stops competing with the actual work of running a gym — helping people get fitter.

## What you can do with it

- **Manage clients** — keep member profiles and history organised in one place.
- **Track training history** — keep a record of every client's sessions and programs over time.
- **Set and follow goals** — agree goals with each client and track how they are moving towards them.
- **See progress at a glance** — clear signals on both client fitness and business health.
- **Handle subscriptions** — set up plans and see at a glance who is active and who is due for renewal.
- **Track payments** — record what has been paid and what is outstanding.
- **Follow sales** — keep an eye on new sign-ups and renewals.
- **Send reminders** — let the system do the remembering, so members hear from you at the right time.
- **Go mobile** — owners get an app to run the business on the move, and clients get their own app to stay connected to their gym.

## Nurturing clients, not just billing them

A membership is more than a payment schedule. MF7 helps owners and trainers look after each client as a person working towards something:

- **Training histories** — every session and program is recorded, so the full story of a client's training stays with the gym, not with whoever happened to coach them.
- **Goals** — set goals with each client and keep them in view, so every session has a purpose.
- **Nurturing** — timely check-ins and nudges tied to where a client is in their journey, not only to when their next payment is due.
- **Clients in the loop** — members see their own history and progress in the MF7 app, which keeps them motivated and coming back.

## Signals you can act on

MF7 turns day-to-day activity into clear signals on the two kinds of progress that matter.

**For clients.** See how each member is moving towards their goals and how consistent they have been over time. Those signals tell trainers when to celebrate a milestone and when to step in before someone drifts away.

**For the business.** See active versus lapsing members, renewals, revenue, and sales trends — so owners know how the month is going without assembling a spreadsheet to find out.

## Why we built it

We build products people use every day, and few businesses are as "every day" as a gym. MF7 exists to take the operational weight off owners so they can spend more of their time on their members and less of it on admin. A gym succeeds when its members do, so MF7 keeps track of both.
