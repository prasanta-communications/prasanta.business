Renting should be simple. You find a place, agree on terms, move in, pay rent, and eventually move on. In practice, every one of those steps tends to come with friction — endless back-and-forth, unclear expectations, and paperwork scattered across chats and inboxes. **Roomr** is our attempt to strip that noise away.

## What Roomr is

Roomr is a rentals product built around one promise: the simplest way to rent. Instead of solving a single slice of the process, it covers the full renting lifecycle, from the start of a rental to its end, so nothing important falls through the gaps between tools.

## Who it is for

Roomr is for the people on both sides of a rental who want things to just work — those looking for a place to live and those who have a place to rent out. If you have ever felt that renting involves more effort than it should, Roomr is built with you in mind.

## The problems it solves

Renting tends to accumulate headaches nobody asked for:

- Information spread across messages, calls, and documents.
- Unclear status — who is responsible for what, and what happens next.
- Repetitive coordination at every stage of a tenancy.

Roomr brings the lifecycle together in one place so the process feels calm and predictable rather than noisy.

## What it covers

- **The full lifecycle** — rather than stopping at discovery, Roomr is designed to stay useful throughout the rental.
- **Less noise** — fewer moving parts, fewer places to check, and a clearer picture of where things stand.
- **Fewer headaches** — removing the small frustrations that make renting feel harder than it is.

## Why we built it

Renting is one of those everyday experiences that almost everyone goes through, and almost everyone has a story about it going wrong. We think the internet can make it far simpler, and Roomr is how we are going about it.
