Every business has a layer of operations that keeps it running — tracking leads, managing members, following up, keeping records straight. It is rarely glamorous, but when it is messy, everything else gets harder. **Workbase HQ** is our suite of tools for exactly this everyday work.

## What Workbase HQ is

Workbase HQ is a growing set of tools for running day-to-day business operations. Rather than trying to be everything at once, we are building it one focused tool at a time, starting with a CRM designed specifically for co-working spaces.

## Why co-working spaces first

Co-working spaces juggle a lot: enquiries from prospective members, tours, desk and cabin bookings, renewals, and the ongoing relationship with the people who work there every day. Generic CRMs are often built for sales teams selling something very different, and they tend to need a lot of bending to fit how a co-working space actually runs.

A CRM built for co-working from the ground up can speak the language of the business from day one.

## Who it is for

The first version of Workbase HQ is for co-working operators and community managers who want a clear view of their leads and members without stitching together spreadsheets, chat threads, and notes. Over time, the suite is meant to grow to serve more kinds of businesses with the same practical approach.

## What it helps with

- **Keeping relationships organised** — leads and members in one place instead of scattered across tools.
- **Staying on top of follow-ups** — fewer opportunities lost to "I meant to get back to them".
- **Seeing the bigger picture** — a clearer view of how the space is doing.

## Why we built it

We run a tight team with a practical stack, and we know how much time operational busywork can eat up. Workbase HQ is our way of packaging what we have learned into tools other businesses can use — starting where we saw a clear, specific need.
