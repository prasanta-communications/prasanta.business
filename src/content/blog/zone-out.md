Most of what we build at Prasanta Communications lives on a screen. **Zone Out** does not. It is a calm luxury fragrance house, and it is our newest launch — built around the idea that a scent can be a small, deliberate escape from a noisy day.

## What Zone Out is

Zone Out creates Extrait de Parfum compositions. Extrait de Parfum is the most concentrated style of fragrance, which typically means a richer, longer-lasting scent that unfolds slowly on the skin. It suits a brand whose whole point is to slow things down.

## The idea behind it

Life is loud — notifications, deadlines, traffic, the constant hum of being reachable. Zone Out is designed for the moments you choose to step out of that: a quiet morning, a walk alone, an evening that belongs only to you. The fragrance becomes a cue, a way of marking those moments as your own.

## Who it is for

Zone Out is for people who see fragrance as more than a finishing touch — those who enjoy a considered, refined scent and value the feeling it creates as much as how it smells. If "calm luxury" sounds like something you want more of, Zone Out is made for you.

## What makes it different

- **Extrait de Parfum** — a high-concentration format chosen for depth and longevity.
- **Calm over loud** — compositions designed to feel composed and understated rather than overpowering.
- **A sense of escape** — every part of the brand is built around leaving the noise behind.

## Why we built it

Our mission is to build products people use every day. A fragrance is about as personal and everyday as products get. Zone Out lets us apply the same care for craft and detail we bring to our software to something entirely sensory.
